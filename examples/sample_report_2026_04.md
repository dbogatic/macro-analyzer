### 2. KEY JUDGMENT

The system is in a late-cycle soft landing. The Fed is on hold — PCE is too close to 3.0% to cut aggressively, but labor is not deteriorating fast enough to force action. The margin for error is thin: one PCE print above 3.0% closes the rate-cut window; one unemployment jump above 4.5% opens the downside break path.

### 3. SCENARIOS

**Controlled Deceleration (40–50%):** Growth continues to slow gradually. PCE drifts toward 2.5%. Fed holds rates through mid-year, then cuts once. Labor market softens but does not break. This is the base case.

**Stabilization / Policy Relief (25–35%):** PCE decelerates faster than expected. Fed gains room to cut 2–3 times. Labor stabilizes. Risk assets re-rate higher. Requires inflation cooperating without a growth accident.

**Downside Break (20–30%):** Core PCE re-accelerates above 3.0%, or unemployment spikes above 4.5%, or both. Fed is caught between a stagflation signal and a labor deterioration signal. Credit spreads widen. Downside scenario probability is elevated relative to historical base rates.

### 4. KEY DRIVERS

- Core PCE trajectory — the single most watched variable. At 2.97% it is one print from changing the entire scenario distribution.
- Labor market momentum — unemployment has been drifting up. Any acceleration signals a regime shift.
- Energy/geopolitical environment — tariff uncertainty is manually weighted at moderate. An escalation to severe would shift the energy/geo module from 19% to ~24%.

### 5. TRIGGERS TO WATCH

- **Core PCE >= 3.0%** — would further constrain Fed flexibility and reduce Stabilization odds
- **Unemployment >= 4.5%** — would raise Downside Break probability and signal labor deterioration
- **HY spread >= 3.89%** (moderate threshold) — would signal financial conditions tightening
- **10Y-2Y spread <= 0.52%** (yield curve flattening warning) — curve at +0.51%, essentially at the threshold

### 6. DOMINANT PATHWAY

Controlled Deceleration with elevated tail risk. The system is not broken but the buffer between the base case and the downside is unusually thin. The next two PCE and payroll prints are decision-critical.

### 7. FINAL SYNTHESIS

Smooth regime does not mean comfortable. Constraint at 2/10 and fragility at 1/10 are low in absolute terms, but both active constraint modules (policy and growth) are pointing in the same direction — toward tightening conditions. The 20–30% Downside Break probability is not a tail risk by conventional standards. This is a hold-and-watch environment, not a risk-on environment.
